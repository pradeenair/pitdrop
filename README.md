# PitDrop

**Pick a pit. Outsmart the board.**

A browser game of shell counting, sowing, and clever turns. Play the system or play locally with another person on the same screen. Open `index.html` in a modern browser; no build step or account is required. Sounds are synthesized in the browser and can be muted.

## Rules

- Choose a board before starting: Classic (7 pits per side, 5 shells each), Deep Pockets (7 pits per side, 7 shells each), or Quick Board (5 pits per side, 5 shells each).
- Pick up all shells from a pit in your row and sow one at a time clockwise. When your hand is empty, continue with the next occupied pit until the pit after your last shell is empty. This is the **Shell Relay**.
- When a pit reaches exactly four shells, the **Four-shell Sweep** sends those shells to the owner of that pit’s row.
- At the **Twin Catch**, collect the shells in the pit beyond the empty pit and in the opposite pit, when present. Then the turn passes.
- At a round end, players refill their own pits in groups matching the selected starting seeds per pit. A pit that cannot be filled is a **Resting Pit** for that round; it may be filled again later. A player who cannot refill any pit loses.

Hand sowing is on by default: after picking a pit, click the glowing next pit or press Space to drop each shell. Use the Hand toggle for automatic sowing. The seven-step guided walkthrough and in-game rules panel teach the game.
